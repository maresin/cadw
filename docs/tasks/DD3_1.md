# D3.1 — TS anomaly — point

*Section:* D — Time Series

**Description:** Detect isolated points that deviate sharply from local behaviour. STATIONARITY NOTE: most detectors (Sub-PCA, EIF, KMeansAD, Autoencoder, PatchMemoryAD, ZMS, STRAY, KShapeAD, DWT-MLEAD, MiniRocket) assume stationary baseline; on trended/seasonal series they produce false positives. Wrap in cadw.ml.anomaly_pipeline. AnomalyPipeline(detector_fn, mode="contextual", seasonal_period=s) to STL-decompose before detection (v0.9.84). Native changepoint detectors (PELT, BOCPD, BinSeg, CUSUM, T-squared+Q) handle trend as signal — use mode="structural" for them.

**Real-life examples:**
- IoT sensor glitch detection
- Transaction fraud in a stream

## Metrics
- **vus_pr** (anchor, higher)
  - Volume Under the Surface (range-aware PR); primary TSAD metric recommended by TSB-AD (NeurIPS 2024) and TAB (VLDB 2025).
  - *special:* Range-aware; resistant to best-of-N seed shopping. Not comparable to point-wise auprc numerically; report which protocol.
- **auprc** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Point-wise area under PR curve.
  - *special:* Point-wise; ignores anomaly range; prefer vus_pr for range-aware anomalies. Not comparable to vus_pr.
- **point_adjusted_f1** (diagnostic, higher)
  - LEGACY — F1 with 1-point tolerance (UCR protocol).
  - *special:* LEGACY and gameable — random ranking hits near-perfect PA-F1 on long series. Reported only for UCR paper comparison; NOT a reliable decision metric.
- **recall** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Fraction of true anomalies flagged; used by CADW cadw_measured synthetic-bench entries.
  - *special:* Affected-ratio dependent (spike × window × 2 / n); recommended_contamination must exceed affected_ratio.
- **vus_roc** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Range-aware ROC-AUC; TSB-AD secondary metric.
  - *special:* Less imbalance-sensitive than VUS-PR but less discriminative; not numerically comparable to point-wise AUC-ROC.
- **range_f1** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Range-aware F1 with tolerance windows (TSB-AD).
  - *special:* Combines precision and recall over anomaly ranges; not comparable to point-wise F1.
- **affiliation_f1** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Affiliation-F1 (TAB/UCR primary for UCR evaluation).
  - *special:* Partial credit for near-miss detections; not comparable to range_f1.
- **auc_roc** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Point-wise ROC-AUC (universal baseline).
  - *special:* Overly optimistic under heavy imbalance (contamination<5%); prefer vus_roc or vus_pr.
- **point_f1** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Point-wise F1 without tolerance (TSB-AD baseline).
  - *special:* Strict; no credit for near-miss detections.
