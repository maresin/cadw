# D3.3 — TS anomaly — shape

*Section:* D — Time Series

**Description:** Detect shape motifs that deviate from the normal repertoire. STATIONARITY NOTE: shape anomalies are typically CONTEXTUAL — a valid shape at the wrong phase/level. On trended or seasonal series, wrap in AnomalyPipeline(detector_fn, mode="contextual", seasonal_period=s) to remove trend+seasonal before shape comparison (v0.9.84). EIF works on both raw windows (structural) and STL residuals (contextual) with different semantics; Sub-PCA/KShapeAD/S2G benefit most from contextual mode.

**Real-life examples:**
- ECG morphology anomalies
- Gesture anomalies

## Metrics
- **vus_pr** (anchor, higher)
  - Volume Under the Surface (window-level PR); primary TSAD metric for range-aware shape anomalies (TSB-AD, TAB).
  - *special:* Range-aware; robust to point-level AUPRC inflation on shape anomalies. Not numerically comparable to point-wise auprc.
- **auprc** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Point-wise area under PR curve at window resolution.
  - *special:* Point-level AUPRC inflates scores on shape anomalies; use for cross-paper UCR comparison only, not as primary.
- **best_5_ranking** (diagnostic, lower)
  - Rank of top-5 detections in ground truth (UCR-style).
  - *special:* Authoritative for UCR paper comparisons; not continuous.
- **vus_roc** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Range-aware ROC-AUC; TSB-AD secondary metric.
  - *special:* Not numerically comparable to point-wise AUC-ROC.
- **range_f1** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Range-aware F1 with tolerance windows (TSB-AD).
  - *special:* Combines precision and recall over anomaly ranges.
- **affiliation_f1** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Affiliation-F1 (TAB/UCR primary for UCR evaluation).
  - *special:* Partial credit for near-miss detections; not comparable to range_f1.
- **auc_roc** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Point-wise ROC-AUC (universal baseline).
  - *special:* Overly optimistic under heavy imbalance; prefer vus_roc.
- **point_f1** (diagnostic, higher)
  - Point-wise F1 without tolerance (TSB-AD baseline).
  - *special:* Strict; no credit for near-miss detections.

## Ensemble rules
- **[local_structure, representation, nonlinear_interactions]** → S2G + Sub-PCA + EIF (max_per_role=1)
  - 3 families: S2G (graph subsequence, uni-only, paper-faithful), Sub-PCA (windowed PCA reconstruction, both uni+multi; multi recall 0.944), EIF (random hyperplane trees, both uni+multi; multi recall 0.864). avg anomaly scores. v0.9.73: Sub-PCA replaced MiniRocket_embedding (uni-only) as multi-capable representation; S2G kept for motif/subsequence on uni, Sub-PCA is the multi-shape workhorse, EIF adds nonlinear_interactions. MiniRocket_embedding, MatrixProfile_discord, zscore, KMeansAD, Autoencoder available as supplementary detectors outside the ensemble.
