## §3. Bimax — honest debt fix

**Before:** `ooc_status: achievable` (v0.9.209 auto-fill).
**Wrong because:** Prelic 2006 Bimax is exponential
`O(r*c*beta^min(r,c))`, beta~1.5. This is algorithmic — no streaming
plan removes exponential search. v0.9.209 auto-fill didn't inspect
algorithm; labeled everything "achievable" if described_only.

**After:** `ooc_status: debt` permanent, `n_scalability: refuse_at_guard`,
`bounded_by_domain {min(r,c): 20}` (honest — at min=20 ~3300 ops OK;
at min=100 ~4e17 infeasible).

**Alternative for larger r,c:** subsample (disclosed) or switch to
PlaidModel (also debt — ALS per layer, polynomial).

**Not a fake plan.** This is honest debt: "algorithm cannot be made
streaming". Different from `achievable` (plan exists, just not implemented).
